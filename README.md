BLOOMWARDEN — Sprout Siege: a bright top-down arena roguelite. You are Wren, keeper of the last Heartbloom. The blight attacks in 8 waves — burn it back with seed bolts, plant sprout-turrets on the soil plots, gather sun and XP sprouts, choose a boon on every level-up, and defeat the Blight Queen to bring back the dawn. 6 enemy kinds plus golden elites, a boss with summons and orb rings, 12 stackable upgrades, combos, dash, synthesized WebAudio sound and music — all offline from static files, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, hold click to fire seeds, Space to dash, E to plant / upgrade a turret, 1 / 2 / 3 to pick a boon, P to pause, M to mute.
